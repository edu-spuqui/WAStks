Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to WAStks (encompassing both the Android mobile application and the companion web tool), developed by Eduardo Spuqui as open-source software. This Service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Overview & Data Collection

WAStks does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal identifiable information or sensitive user data on external servers.

2. WAStks (Android Application)

Local Data Processing

Information processed by the mobile app (such as sticker pack details, package publisher names, or asset lists) remains stored strictly on your local device. No personal data or statistics are ever transmitted off your device.

Permissions & Foreground Services

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Requested solely to display progress notifications when a foreground service is actively downloading sticker assets requested by the user. No notification data is collected or shared.

3. WAStks (Locally Hosted Web Tool)

Local File Storage

The web tool processes configurations and saves data (such as Owner Name, Device Name, and Pack Publisher) exclusively into local files on the host machine. This information is never uploaded or transmitted to any external remote server.

Cookies & Local Browser Storage

The tool uses browser cookies and local storage exclusively to save non-personal user preferences and navigation state, including:

  • Language preference
  • UI Theme (Light/Dark)
  • Image Quality settings
  • Last Accessed Package

4. Third-Party Services & Log Data

WAStks does not integrate third-party analytics libraries, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or crash reporting services. We do not collect Log Data (such as IP addresses or device telemetry).

5. Security

Because all data remains strictly local on your device or machine, your information is protected by your device's own security features.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page and are effective immediately upon posting.
